# 🐅 VANYA SAFARI — National Park Booking Website

**Vanya Safari** is a multi-page website for exploring four of India's famous national parks and booking a jungle safari. Visitors can read about each park, study its safari zones on schematic maps, choose services such as jeep or canter safaris and night stays, and fill in a booking and payment form.

This project was built as part of the **CSE326** course.

## 📖 About the Project

A jungle safari is more than a visit to a national park; it is a chance to step out of everyday life and experience the wild. Vanya Safari brings the information a visitor needs into one place: what each park is known for, which zones to visit, what services are available, and how to book them.

---

## ✨ Features

### 🏠 Home Page
- Welcome section explaining why a jungle safari is worth the trip
- Overview of the four national parks with a link to each park's page
- Available services:
  - **Private Jeep Safari**: a dedicated Gypsy vehicle for family or friends
  - **Shared Canter Safari**: an affordable option shared with other visitors
  - **Night Stay & Accommodation**: an overnight stay inside the park surroundings, with meals charged separately
  - **Wild Animal Interaction**: a supervised experience, subject to animal availability, park permissions and safety rules
- Important visitor information and safety guidelines

### 🌿 Park Pages
One page for each park, in the same layout:

| Park | State | Page |
|------|-------|------|
| Jim Corbett National Park | Uttarakhand | `jcnp.html` |
| Ranthambore National Park | Rajasthan | `rnp.html` |
| Kaziranga National Park | Assam | `knp.html` |
| Bandhavgarh National Park | Madhya Pradesh | `bnp.html` |

### 🗺️ Zone Maps
- Schematic maps of all four parks showing **core zones, buffer zones, water bodies and animal hotspots**
- A legend and a list of where to look for each species
- The maps are approximate and not to scale; the park's official zone map should be used for navigation or booking

### 🧾 Booking & Payment Page
- **Guest & stay details:** name, email, phone, number of people, visit date and night-stay option
- **Transportation:** personal jeep or shared bus
- **Animal interaction permit** with the safety rules and a per-person animal visit fee
- **Service pricing** (per person)
- **Payment details** and a *Confirm & Pay* button

> ⚠️ This is an academic project. Please do not enter real card details on the demo booking form.

## 🚀 How to Run

**Online:** open the [live website](https://rohansk09omiy-source.github.io/CSE326-VanyaSafari/HTMLproject/htmlcode.html).

**Locally:**
1. Clone the repository
   ```bash
   git clone https://github.com/rohansk09omiy-source/CSE326-VanyaSafari.git
   ```
2. Open the `HTMLproject` folder.
3. Double-click `htmlcode.html` to open the home page in your browser.

No installation or server is needed; it is a static website.

## 📜 Visitor Guidelines (from the website)

- Follow the instructions of authorised forest officials and guides
- Maintain silence and avoid disturbing wild animals
- Do not feed, touch or approach wild animals unless an authorised programme permits it
- Carry valid identification documents for your booking
- Follow the designated safari routes and timings
