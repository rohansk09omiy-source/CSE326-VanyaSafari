About Ranthambore National Park

Where Tigers Meet Ancient Ruins

Located in the Sawai Madhopur district of Rajasthan, Ranthambore National Park is one of India’s most famous tiger reserves. It is known for rocky hills, dry deciduous forest and some of the best chances in the country of seeing a tiger in the open.

The park’s backdrop is a historic hilltop fort, with ruins, temples and old stone structures scattered through the forest and along the edges of its lakes.

Ranthambore is a Project Tiger reserve. Its safari area is divided into ten zones: Zones 1 to 5 form the core, and Zones 6 to 10 are the quieter buffer zones.

From tigers and leopards to sloth bears, striped hyenas and many birds, Ranthambore offers a memorable wildlife experience in a truly dramatic setting.

Discover the Beauty of Ranthambore

Ranthambore Fort

An ancient fort stands above the forest, and its ruins, gateways and temples give the park a unique historic setting.

Historic Lakes

Lakes such as Padam Talao and Rajbagh Talao attract wildlife and reflect the old ruins along their shores.

Forest and Rocky Hills

Dry deciduous forest, open meadows and rugged hills create varied habitats for many species.

Best Time to Visit Ranthambore

Ranthambore changes through the year. Your best season depends on the weather, the scenery and the kind of wildlife experience you want.

Winter Season

October – February

The park reopens after the monsoon, with fresh greenery in October and cool, comfortable mornings through winter. Layered clothing helps on early safaris.

Ideal for:

Families, photographers and first-time visitors.

Summer Season

March – June

Summer is hot, and animals often visit lakes and water sources more frequently, although sightings are never guaranteed.

Ideal for:

Visitors focused on wildlife observation.

Monsoon Season

July – September

The forest turns green, but the core zones close during these months. Buffer zones may operate, depending on weather and forest conditions.

Ideal for:

Quiet landscapes where access is allowed.

Visitor note: Zone opening dates and safari timings change by season. Always check the latest official schedule before planning your safari.

Explore the Safari Zones

Ranthambore has ten safari zones. Each safari is assigned to a particular zone, so it helps to know what each one offers.

Safari Zone Type Known For Visiting Period
Zone 1 Core Lake scenery and historic ruins October – June*
Zone 3 Core Lake scenery, ruins and frequent tiger sightings October – June*
Zones 2, 4 and 5 Core Lakes, rocky terrain and high tiger density October – June*
Zones 6 to 10 Buffer Wider forest tracks, open meadows, leopards, striped hyenas, sloth bears and birds Buffer zones may open in monsoon*

*Dates are subject to official permissions, weather and safety conditions, and may change. Confirm on the official booking portal.
